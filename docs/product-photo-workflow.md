# TRAUMA product photo workflow

This is the owner's required workflow for all current and future product photos. It overrides any generic visual-design or image-generation suggestions for garment imagery.

## Preserve the actual garment

Use only real TRAUMA garments and owner-supplied photographs. Do not redesign products, alter artwork, modify graphics, change garment colors, remove logos, generate mockups, invent missing details, or fabricate product views. Preserve text, print placement, logos, fabric texture, silhouette, distressing, patchwork, seams, and the garment's real appearance.

Retain original uploads unchanged. Work on separate copies and preserve the best available source resolution. Never regenerate a garment merely because its file or a requested view is unavailable. Never substitute an unrelated product image.

## Allowed edits only

- Background removal or background cleanup.
- White or light gray background replacement.
- Lighting correction and brightness/contrast adjustment without changing actual garment colors.
- Shadow cleanup.
- Image sharpening without changing printed details.
- Centering and cropping without cutting off the garment or obscuring its artwork.

Remove background clutter, not details belonging to the garment. Use consistent professional framing and backgrounds. Do not invent hangers, accessories, product details, extra lettering, or missing garment regions. Preserve natural, accurate colors rather than stylizing them.

## Workflow for each upload

1. Identify the real product and confirm that usable original image files are accessible. If chat previews are visible but original files or download references are unavailable, state that limit and request the original files or real image URLs. Do not claim an image was processed or saved when it was not.
2. Preserve originals and create separate presentation copies using only the allowed adjustments.
3. Check the result against the originals: garment shape, actual color, artwork, wording, logo, print placement, stitching, patches, and material details must remain faithful. Reject an output that changes the garment or graphics.
4. Build the product gallery from the supplied views only. Prefer front as cover, then back, then the actual supplied tag/detail/hood/sleeve views as appropriate. Never synthesize missing views or use placeholder photographs.
5. Use responsive, uncropped garment presentation on mobile and desktop. Keep original files available and derive optimized delivery sizes separately. Do not upscale or over-sharpen to manufacture detail.
6. Add only owner-supplied product information. Keep unknown details blank and retain `published: false` until the owner approves publication. Photo cleanup approval does not imply product publication or website deployment approval.

## Gallery and review

Generate the gallery layout, thumbnails, and navigation from actual approved image files; the gallery itself must not generate inventory or replace the real garment with a mockup. Present the cleaned images and draft product record for review before publishing. Maintain apparel purchase links through the owner's actual Depop/PayPal destinations and books/digital links through Payhip.

No image processing or new product images were created when these rules were saved. Previously prepared records that have no usable photo files still have empty photo arrays and remain unpublished.

## Updated owner photography rules

Noise reduction and image resizing for web performance are also allowed, provided they preserve actual garment appearance. Never change embroidery, print placement, distressing, fabric appearance, branding, existing design elements, or actual garment colors. Never add or remove a design. Authenticity is more important than perfection, and the customer must receive the product actually shown.

Quick Add uploads retain original image bytes. The storefront's responsive image delivery makes separate web-size derivatives. The uploader currently does not automatically remove backgrounds, relight photographs, denoise, or sharpen them; do not claim those operations happened. A photo-processing integration and comparison/review step would be needed before promising that automation. No cleanup service is configured, and no background, artwork, garment, or color is silently regenerated.
