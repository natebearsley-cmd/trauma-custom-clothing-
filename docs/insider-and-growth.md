# TRAUMA growth release runbook

## Open Insider Club

1. In your email platform, create a dedicated TRAUMA Insider Club list with a hosted HTTPS form endpoint. Enable double opt-in, provider-side abuse controls, unsubscribe, and a valid business mailing address in the email footer. Review the provider's privacy/data settings. Do not put emails in GitHub, analytics events, or checkout URLs.
2. The form POST sends `email`, `marketing_consent=yes`, and `source`. Map the source as a field or equivalent tag in the provider. If your provider uses different field names or requires a hidden list token or JS, adapt the form before connecting it. Test in a private preview with an address you control; check receipt, confirmation, list membership, source, and unsubscribe.
3. Add the provider's public form action as `INSIDER_FORM_ACTION` to the TRAUMA Vercel project and redeploy. Existing `WAITLIST_FORM_ACTION` is the fallback only if it accepts the same payload and confirms opt-in. Never put a private API key in either form URL. The app leaves signup and site-wide calls to action closed without a valid endpoint. Seven QR PNGs encode the canonical production domain; regenerate and decode them if the official domain changes.
4. On iPhone, check the homepage, a product page, the PayPal checkout review, footer, and menu. Each QR has a tappable equivalent. The existing single-item checkout review is the cart-stage placement. `/order-follow-up` can be added to an approved receipt or verified return flow. It does not confirm payment; the marketplace still owns the real receipt and the Payhip download flow. Prefer a receipt message over a global Payhip redirect if digital download access would be interrupted. No marketplace receipt or redirect was changed by this release.

## Activate the 10% welcome offer

The discount remains hidden until signup works and a qualifying product ID is configured. Before adding an ID to `INSIDER_DISCOUNT_PRODUCT_IDS`, confirm the piece is full-price, repeatable apparel, has at least two measured inventory units, supports an approved direct PayPal invoice, and clears the contribution floor after 10% off. No current site item passes the structured repeatable-stock check.

- Discount eligible merchandise on a direct invoice before shipping and tax. No stacking, digital items, Depop/Payhip/TikTok orders, sale items, one-of-one pieces or custom quotes.
- Check the customer's email against confirmed subscribers and a private redemption log before sending the invoice. Record invoice ID, redemption date, and offer version; never make the discount dependent on entering or winning a giveaway. Keep the current shipping quote workflow.
- Keep platform offer settings off for one-of-one pieces. For repeatable stock, consider automation only after measuring item, labor, packaging, seller-paid shipping, flat fees, return allowance, and variable fees. In TRAUMA Admin use Offer Guardrails. Depop auto-counters lower offers at the configured floor; it cannot run the requested manual midrange bucket on the same listing.

## TikTok and weekly reporting

- Keep each physical one-of-one tied to one internal SKU across channels. Compare all Seller Center statuses, including drafts and inactive or rejected listings. Match seller SKU first, then exact photo, base, size, condition, artwork and available unit. Update an existing match, use genuine variations under an existing parent where allowed, and hold ambiguous matches. Seller Center is authoritative; Prep status is manual.
- Existing videos: in TikTok Shop Creator Center use **Toolkit → Link products** or **Videos**. Use only an approved, available listing for the exact garment shown. The published-video tool generally covers eligible posts from the previous 30 days; check music eligibility and current options in the app. Do not replace a sold one-of-one with a different piece.
- Store exports outside the repo. Normalize the headers listed by `python3 scripts/growth-report.py --help`. Use one report window, all product statuses, one row per video, and aggregate signup counts only. Example: `python3 scripts/growth-report.py --products /private/products.csv --complete-products --videos /private/videos.csv --complete-videos --metrics /private/metrics.csv --subscribers /private/signups.csv --window "Last 28 days" > /private/trauma-report.json`. Mark completeness only when verified. The tool reports unmatched candidates, never authorizes creation.
- Review confirmed new and lost subscribers by week and source; keep unknown source separate. Rank TikTok by paid orders attributed to product clicks divided by product clicks, same period and attribution basis. The report leaves samples under 30 clicks unranked. Separately track net revenue and contribution after returns. Depop public sold counts are not a conversion rate.

## Community and giveaways

Before a campaign, verify the recipient's status, obtain written permission for its name/logo and record who collects funds, eligible SKUs/dates, the calculation basis, caps or minimums, return treatment, jurisdiction requirements, and payout deadline. Keep a private ledger and receipts. Post verified totals after settlement: sales/counts in scope, calculation, amount, recipient and payout date. Redact buyer data. Send donors directly to a verified organization when possible. Leave Gives Back in its inactive state until a real campaign is documented.

For a drawing, obtain review for the intended jurisdictions before launch. Publish finalized rules before entry opens. Consider a limited Illinois pilot. Subscriber entry must be clearly disclosed and voluntary; provide a free entry form with no purchase, subscription, or other material burden and equal odds. One entry per person regardless of route. Make future draws optional, preserve valid entries for the current draw after unsubscribe, and separate promotional consent from free entry. Keep the drawing and any monthly promise off until the rules, prize, alternate form, winner workflow and approvals are ready.
